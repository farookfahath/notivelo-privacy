Notivelo Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Developer: Farook Fahath, Sri Lanka

Notivelo is free for everyone. It is an independent Android notification-history app designed to keep selected notifications on your own device. This policy explains how the Android app handles information and the choices available to you.

1. What Notivelo does

Notivelo saves notification information supplied by Android from the apps you select, so you can view, search, save, and organize that local history. It does not read WhatsApp or other source-app databases, use Android Accessibility services, retrieve private chats, or recover messages that Android did not provide through notifications.

2. Notification access and user consent

Notivelo explains notification access before directing you to Android Settings. You must consent and enable access yourself in Android Settings; the app does not grant itself access. You also choose exactly which apps may be captured. No source app is selected by default.

Android notification access is a broad system permission: Android may deliver callbacks for apps you have not selected. Notivelo checks your selected-app settings before parsing or storing their notification content. You can change those selections or revoke notification access in Android Settings at any time. Capture depends on consent, system access, your selections, and Android's listener availability.

3. What information is stored

For selected apps, Notivelo stores only notification information Android provides. This may include the source app name and package, title or sender, message or preview text, timestamp, and conversation or notification-lifecycle metadata when supplied by Android. Sender names and message text can themselves contain personal or sensitive information. Notivelo does not independently verify or obtain additional information about a sender.

The app also keeps local information needed for its features, such as consent and app selections, appearance and privacy settings, saved status, local categories and rules, and technical notification identifiers used to organize history and avoid duplicates. These records and settings stay on your device. Notivelo does not log notification contents for analytics or send them to the developer.

4. What is not collected

Notivelo has no account, cloud sync, Firebase, ads, tracking, analytics, advertising-ID use, or notification uploads. The developer does not receive your notification history, app selections, or local settings through the app. The Android app does not declare the INTERNET permission, and its core functionality works offline.

Notivelo does not retrieve source-app databases, private chats, contact lists, phone numbers, email addresses, passwords, or files from other apps. A notification you choose to capture may itself contain such information; that notification content is handled locally as described above. Notivelo does not use Accessibility to inspect other apps.

If you voluntarily email support, the developer receives the email and whatever information you include. Do not send notification history, passwords, authentication codes, or other sensitive information unless you choose to share it and it is necessary for your question. Support correspondence is not automatic app telemetry.

5. Local storage and security

Notification history is stored locally in an app-private Room database on the device. Settings are also kept in app-private local storage. Android app-private storage separates this data from ordinary access by other apps. Automatic Android backup/device transfer of Notivelo's private data is excluded by the app's backup configuration.

The live Room database does not have separate database-level encryption added by Notivelo. Encrypted backup files do not encrypt the live database. Android's device security and storage protections, your device screen lock, and Notivelo's optional app lock provide additional protection. These protections have practical limits, particularly on a compromised, rooted, or unlocked device. No storage or screen-protection measure is an absolute guarantee.

6. App lock and screen protection

The optional app lock uses Android biometric authentication or your device credentials, such as the device PIN, pattern, or password. Notivelo does not store your biometric data or a separate app-lock password. Authentication is handled by Android. The lock protects access to Notivelo's interface; it is not a claim of separate database encryption. Notification capture can continue independently of the interface lock.

When the app's existing privacy rules require protected screens, Notivelo uses Android secure-screen controls to reduce screenshots and recent-app previews. Screen protection is a privacy mitigation, not an absolute guarantee against every possible capture method, external camera, or compromised system.

7. Retention, saved items, pause, and deletion

You can pause capture, resume it, change selected apps, delete individual records, clear all history, and configure automatic retention. Saved notifications are protected from automatic retention cleanup. Explicit deletion or clearing history can remove saved items too.

Pausing capture or removing an app from your selections stops future capture under those settings; it does not delete already stored history. Resuming capture does not recover notifications missed during a pause or unavailable listener period. Revoking Android notification access stops new capture while existing local history remains available.

Deleting history removes the stored notification records, including their message text and titles. Local opaque deduplication identifiers may remain to prevent repeated notification updates from restoring deleted records. Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling removes the app's local storage, including these identifiers and settings, subject to Android's behavior. Deleting records does not automatically delete copies you previously exported, copied, or shared. You manage those copies separately.

8. Optional local backup/import and its limitations

Notivelo offers optional passphrase-protected encrypted local backup/export and import. Export and import occur only when you choose them. Notivelo does not upload backups to its own server or cloud, and no server-side recovery service exists.

You choose the backup file location through Android's file picker. If you choose a location managed by another app or a cloud-storage provider, that provider may copy or sync the file according to its own settings and policy. That behavior is outside Notivelo's local backup feature.

Keep the passphrase and exported files safe. Notivelo cannot recover a forgotten backup passphrase. Encryption of exported files does not protect copies of plain text you separately copy or share, does not add encryption to the live database, and does not erase previous exported copies. Import can restore information that you previously deleted from the app if it remains in a backup you choose to import.

9. Android and source-app preview limitations

Notivelo can store only notification content Android supplies. Android or the source app may provide shortened previews, hidden or redacted content, or incomplete metadata. Notivelo cannot recover missing text, retrieve private chats, or reconstruct a full message from a shortened preview. A source app may not post a notification at all, and Android or device battery-management behavior can interrupt delivery. Notification history is not a complete chat backup.

10. External links

The Privacy Policy, LinkedIn, and optional Buy Me a Coffee links open in your device's browser. Notivelo does not embed a WebView or handle payments. Support is optional; all Notivelo features remain free regardless of donations.

Those websites and services have their own privacy policies and may handle information through your browser when you visit them. GitHub hosts this public policy page and applies its own policies to visits. Notivelo does not send notification history or app settings with these links. Any information you choose to submit to a third-party website is governed by that service's policy.

11. Children's privacy

Notivelo does not ask for your age, create children's profiles, or automatically transmit children's notification information to the developer. Notifications can contain information about people of any age, including children, and selected notification content is stored locally under the same rules described in this policy. Users and caregivers should consider which apps are selected and who can access the device. Contact the developer with questions or concerns about privacy.

12. Policy changes

This policy may be updated when Notivelo's features or information-handling practices change. The public policy page will show the updated effective date. Material changes to access or consent will be explained through appropriate app disclosures; an updated policy does not itself grant notification access or select apps for you.
